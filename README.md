# Great Question for Cursor

Run your user research from inside Cursor. Ask what customers said about the flow you're building, pull quotes for a pull request, or set up and recruit a study to test it, without leaving your editor.

## What you can do

- Find what customers said about a feature, a flow or a problem, across every study
- Pull the most common complaints or requests, with direct quotes
- Read transcripts, highlights, screener responses, and survey and prototype results
- Create interviews, surveys and prototype tests, with screeners and incentives
- Shortlist participants from your panel and send screener invitations
- Set interview availability and add moderators

Your existing Great Question permissions apply, so Cursor only sees what you can see.

## Before you start

You need a Great Question account with MCP turned on. The MCP is included with Enterprise plans. An admin switches it on in Settings, under AI & MCP.

## Install

1. Open **Customize** in Cursor's sidebar.
2. Find **Great Question** in the marketplace and select **Install**.
3. Connect Great Question and sign in with your Great Question account. There are no API keys to copy.

## Skills included

The plugin comes with six starter skills that show Cursor how to do research work well with Great Question: writing screeners, recruiting participants, setting incentives, synthesizing findings, setting up a study, and writing to participants.

If your admins have written their own skills in Great Question, Cursor gets those too when you connect, and it follows yours over ours. Learn more: https://greatquestion.co/support/integrations/mcp-skills

## Try asking

- What have customers said about our checkout in the last six months?
- Give me the three most common complaints about our current dashboard, with direct quotes.
- Set up an interview study on why new users drop off during onboarding: 8 participants, $100 incentive each.
